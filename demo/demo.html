XERF (Skin Tightening)

XERF is the newest skin tightening treatment at the spa, and it works without needles or downtime. It treats aging by restoring lost and broken collagen, using radiofrequency energy to heat the deeper layers of skin. That heat tells the body to build fresh collagen, elastin, and hyaluronic acid, which over weeks firms the face and body. The treatment suits people who notice lax skin along the jaw, neck, or arms and want a change without surgery. Sessions take about an hour, and most people return to normal life the same day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will it hurt?

Most people feel a small pinch that lasts a second. The needles are very fine, and the whole treatment takes only minutes. A numbing cream is rarely needed, though the provider can use ice to ease the pinch. You can go back to work right after, though the team asks you to skip heavy exercise and lying flat for a few hours.

Will I look frozen?

Not with the approach this spa takes. The injectors use small amounts and aim for a soft, rested look, so you can still raise your brows and smile. Tell the provider exactly what you want at the consult: some people want every line gone, others want a light touch. The plan is built around your answer, not a standard dose.

How long will it last?

Most people enjoy the result for three to four months. The effect builds over three to seven days, and the full result shows at about two weeks. That is why the team books a follow-up at two weeks: they can check the result and add a small touch-up if one side needs it. Regular visits can stretch the effect a little longer over time.

What areas can Botox treat?

The most common areas are the forehead, the lines between the brows, and the crow's feet at the eyes. It can also soften a gummy smile, calm a jaw that clenches, and smooth the lines around the mouth in some cases. Your injector will look at how your muscles move when you talk and smile, then suggest the areas that will help most.

Can I combine Botox with laser or microneedling?

Yes, and many clients do, but the order and timing matter. Botox is often done first or spaced a week or two from laser and microneedling so each treatment can settle. Your provider will map out the order at your consult and explain why a gap helps. Never stack treatments on the same day without asking the team first.

What should I avoid after Botox?

Skip heavy exercise, saunas, and alcohol for a day, and do not press, rub, or massage the treated area. Stay upright for a few hours after the injections. The team gives you a short written list so you do not have to remember it all, and they will answer questions by phone if anything feels odd.
